# Letters to the three trusts — draft

**Status: draft. Nothing here has been sent.** Read it as a starting point, change the voice to
your own, and see "Before you send" at the foot.

The single largest gap in the map is that 87
of the 88 sites have no location of their own and are drawn at
their town's centre. Open map data cannot close this — OpenStreetMap holds two Rajchandra-named
features in all of Gujarat, both hospitals. The three bodies below run the yatras to these sites
and know where they are. One letter each is the fastest route to closing it.

Each letter asks for one thing and makes it cheap to answer. Do not ask for endorsement, and do
not imply any; the map's own footer disclaims it, and saying so plainly is what makes the request
answerable.

## Before you send

**Have the Gujarati reader translate these.** The ashram offices at Vadva, Idar and Sayla work in
Gujarati, and a letter in Gujarati from a devotee will be read differently from an English one. The
same person doing the Gujarati reading of `review.html` is the right person to do it — and asking
them to is a natural way to open that conversation.

**The Trust's own contact page has a typo in its email addresses.** It prints
`admin@shimadrajchandratrust.org` and `info@shimadrajchandratrust.org` — *shimad*, missing the r.
That domain has no mail server at all, so anything sent there vanishes. `srnm1viharbhavantrust@gmail.com`
is the address to use. The correctly spelled `shrimadrajchandratrust.org` does have a mail server,
so `info@` there is worth a try as a second attempt. Their telephone numbers are Ahmedabad
079-26631396, Vadva 02698 220631, Idar 02778 251357 — and for an ashram office a phone call will
very likely outrun email.

Raj Saubhag list Mr Harivadan Brahmbhatt on +91 2755 280533 / +91 9426259111. SRMD's ashram line is
+91 7285040000, 9am–9pm IST.

**The first letter is long, and that is deliberate — but it can be split.** Sixty-five sites is a
lot to put in front of someone at once. If it goes unanswered, a second attempt naming five or six
sites in a single district, or a phone call about one yatra route, is far more likely to land than
a reminder about the whole list.

**Send `review.html` only if they ask.** Leading with it makes the letter a request for an audit,
which is a much larger thing to say yes to than "where is this building."

**These lists go stale as answers come in.** `python build.py --unlocated` reprints them from the
current data, grouped the same way, so a second round can name only what is still missing. It does
not touch this file — the letters above are yours to edit, and nothing regenerates over them.

**Log what comes back in the CSVs, not in your inbox.** A reply naming a building goes into
`data/sites.csv` — coordinates in `lat`/`lng`, a published address in `address`, and the trust and
the date in `evidence_note` so the claim keeps its provenance. Then `python build.py`.
